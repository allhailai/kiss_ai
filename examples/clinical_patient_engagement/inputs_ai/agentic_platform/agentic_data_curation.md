## Summary

Agentic data curation can keep engagement knowledge and operational context current by collecting sources, classifying evidence strength, summarizing outcomes, identifying open questions, and routing review items to humans.

## Key Findings

- The project requirements explicitly call for agentic data curation alongside patient interaction strategies.
- Cross-industry engagement platforms rely on unified customer profiles, event history, segmentation, and analytics for personalization.
- In healthcare, agents should curate data with provenance, auditability, review queues, and safeguards against unsupported claims.
- Curation agents can support staff by turning engagement attempts into structured outcomes and learning loops.

## Practical Tactics or Workflow Patterns

- Classify sources as independent evidence, healthcare case study, vendor claim, cross-industry example, expert opinion, compliance source, or internal context.
- Maintain an evidence-strength field and source date for each recommendation.
- Use agents to detect stale sources, unresolved questions, and changes that may affect outputs.

## Transferability Assessment

Customer data platform and knowledge-base maintenance patterns transfer if privacy and provenance are enforced.

## Operational Implications

The platform should include source registry, review queues, evidence labels, and output provenance.

## Compliance, Privacy, Safety, Trust, and Accessibility Caveats

Do not let agents silently update conclusions without human-visible change logs for material changes.

## Limitations

This is an initial internet-informed baseline. It combines public evidence, public guidance, public vendor pages, and strategy synthesis. Vendor claims are not treated as independent proof of effectiveness.
