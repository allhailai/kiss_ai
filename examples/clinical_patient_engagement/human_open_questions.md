## Open Questions

1. Which patient populations or service lines should this project prioritize first?
   Will be targeting Medicaid populations, which include Dual eligible (Medicare & Medicaid) as well as Medicaid only.
   This population will likely be in the Medicaid MLTSS HCBS waivers, which I believe is under the ABD population
2. Are there specific compliance constraints, consent rules, brand rules, or platform limitations the engagement workflows must honor?
   HIPAA. There will be sensitivity around patient consent and HCBS CareGiver (non-clinical supportive care) consents. We will be engaging both patients and their caregivers.

3. Should the first outputs focus on general strategy, operational playbooks, or channel-specific scripts/templates?
   General strategies and operational playbooks. Any scripts and templates are finery that should be considered but put into more detailed outputs, example files etc... we don't want to clutter up the project with detail when we are first focusing on strategy.
4. For patient-facing agentic engagement, which channel pattern should be prioritized first for Medicaid, dual eligible, ABD/MLTSS/HCBS patients and caregivers?
   Candidate patterns to compare: AI voice/agentic phone calls, SMS with consent, SMS notification linking to secure messaging in a mobile or secure web app, secure portal/app messaging, and mixed AI-to-human callback workflows. This does not block strategy generation, but it should be resolved before implementation.
