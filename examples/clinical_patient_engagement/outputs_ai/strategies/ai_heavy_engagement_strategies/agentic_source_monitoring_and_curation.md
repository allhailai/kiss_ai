## Target Segment or Problem

Knowledge-base and strategy maintenance for a fast-changing engagement platform and vendor landscape.

## Automation Opportunity and Boundaries

Agents can monitor sources, classify evidence, summarize changes, flag stale claims, and route material changes to humans. They should not silently change recommendations when material evidence or compliance assumptions shift.

## Data Needed by the Agent

Source URLs, evidence type, last_checked date, topic mapping, vendor capability tags, and open questions.

## Data Generated by the Agent

Source summaries, change flags, evidence labels, stale-source warnings, and proposed wiki/output updates.

## Monitoring and QA Plan

Review material changes, sample summaries, require citations, and log every recommendation-changing update.
