## Target Segment or Problem

Low-risk missed appointments or unconfirmed appointments where the goal is to confirm, reschedule, or request a human callback.

## Automation Opportunity and Boundaries

AI voice may handle scripted first-touch recovery and collect structured outcomes. It should not provide clinical advice, resolve consent ambiguity, or handle distress.

## Vendor Capabilities Needed

Voice generation, VOIP/calling, transcription, structured outcome capture, routing, audit logs, opt-out handling, and live handoff.

## Human Review and Escalation

Escalate to staff for distress, confusion, caregiver complexity, repeated non-response, clinical questions, complaints, or opt-out requests.

## Monitoring and QA Plan

Sample transcripts, review failed calls, track escalation accuracy, and monitor opt-outs and complaints.

## Caveats

AI-generated voice calls require careful TCPA, disclosure, consent, HIPAA, and governance review.
