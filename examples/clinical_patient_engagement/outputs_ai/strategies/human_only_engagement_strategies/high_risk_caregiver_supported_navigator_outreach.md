# High-Risk Caregiver-Supported Navigator Outreach

## Sources Relied On

- `inputs_ai/populations/segmentation_accessibility_trust.md`
- `inputs_ai/workflows/clinical_outreach_workflows.md`
- `outputs_ai/wiki/topics/patient-segmentation.md`

## Target Segment or Problem

Medicaid, dual eligible, ABD, MLTSS, or HCBS members where caregiver involvement, service complexity, disability, language access, or low trust makes automation risky.

## Why Human-Led

A live navigator can verify consent, clarify caregiver role, respond empathetically, and resolve ambiguity that AI should not handle independently.

## Workflow Steps

1. Review patient/caregiver context, consent, language, and accessibility needs.
2. Call patient or authorized caregiver.
3. Explain purpose plainly and confirm preferred channel.
4. Resolve the immediate engagement need or schedule next step.
5. Document outcome and any consent/caregiver updates.

## Measurement Plan

Track successful contact, resolved need, escalation reason, patient/caregiver satisfaction signals, and staff time.

## Caveats

Requires staffing capacity and clear caregiver consent rules.
