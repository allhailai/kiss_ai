## Template 1: Appointment Reminder and Recovery

1. Verify consent, preferred language, caregiver role, and channel preference.
2. Send SMS or portal reminder with confirm/reschedule action.
3. If no response, send second reminder or AI voice first-touch if policy allows.
4. If still no response or high-risk flag exists, route to live navigator.
5. Record outcome and next action.

## Template 2: Care Plan Follow-Up

1. Segment by risk, need, and caregiver involvement.
2. Use human-approved message or call prompt.
3. Let AI classify replies into complete, needs help, confused, declined, safety concern, or unreachable.
4. Route exceptions to staff.
5. Close the loop with documentation and next step.

## Template 3: Caregiver-Supported Engagement

1. Confirm authorization and communication preference.
2. Provide plain-language purpose and role expectations.
3. Use human-led outreach when relationship context or consent is unclear.
4. Document caregiver outcome separately from patient outcome where appropriate.
