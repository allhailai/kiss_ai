## Near-Term Opportunities

- AI reply classification into work queues.
- AI-generated SMS drafts with approval rules.
- AI voice first-touch for low-risk scripted reminders.
- SMS-to-secure-message links for PHI-sensitive or multi-step patient/caregiver workflows.
- Channel-comparison pilots across AI voice, SMS, secure messaging, and human callback.
- Outreach timeline and patient context panel.
- Consent-aware routing and escalation.
- Agentic source monitoring and evidence classification.

## Do Not Automate Yet

- Clinical decision-making.
- Distress, complaints, or safety concerns.
- Ambiguous caregiver consent.
- Sensitive relationship repair.

## Platform Features

- Work queues.
- Outreach timeline.
- Patient and caregiver context panel.
- Secure message launch and completion tracking.
- Channel comparison dashboard.
- Approval queue.
- Prompt/script versioning.
- QA review and audit logs.
- Measurement dashboard.
