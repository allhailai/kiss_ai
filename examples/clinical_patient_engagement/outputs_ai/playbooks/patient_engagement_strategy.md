## Recommended Strategy

Focus first on Medicaid, dual eligible, ABD, MLTSS, and HCBS populations. Build general strategy and operational playbooks before detailed scripts. Use a segmented, closed-loop engagement model that combines SMS, phone, mail, portal/email, AI-supported routing, and human escalation.

## Execution Model

- Use SMS for low-risk reminders, confirmations, and simple next actions.
- Use live human calls for low-trust, high-risk, caregiver-heavy, sensitive, or repeated non-response situations.
- Use AI-heavy workflows for scale in reminders, routing, reply classification, summarization, and data curation.
- Use mixed workflows when AI can draft, classify, or first-touch, while humans approve or resolve exceptions.

## Review Requirements

Compliance, clinical operations, and privacy leaders should review consent, caregiver/proxy communication, HIPAA, AI voice, opt-out, and minimum necessary content before implementation.

## Measurement Plan

Track reachability, response, confirmation, completion, no-show reduction, care gap closure, escalation volume, opt-outs, complaints, staff burden, and safety events.

## Open Questions or Blockers

- Confirm the specific ABD, MLTSS, and HCBS service lines for the first pilot.
- Confirm caregiver consent and non-clinical supportive care communication rules.
