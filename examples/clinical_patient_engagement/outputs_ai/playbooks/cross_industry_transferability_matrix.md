# Cross-Industry Transferability Matrix

## Sources Relied On

- `inputs_ai/cross_industry_examples/transferable_engagement_patterns.md`
- `outputs_ai/wiki/topics/cross-industry-engagement-patterns.md`

| Pattern | Source Industry | Clinical Transfer | Caveat |
|---|---|---|---|
| Omnichannel continuity | Retail, subscription, contact centers | High | Must protect PHI and preserve consent context |
| Proactive reminders | Consumer apps, travel | High | Avoid pressure and over-messaging |
| Next-best-action | Fintech, e-commerce | Medium | Must be explainable and clinically governed |
| Warm transfer | Contact centers | High | Critical for trust-sensitive escalation |
| Churn prevention | Subscriptions | Medium | Translate to care continuity, not marketing retention |
| Personalization | Consumer technology | Medium | Avoid stigma, manipulation, or privacy leakage |

## Recommendation

Use cross-industry patterns as design prompts, not evidence of clinical effectiveness.
