# Engagement Measurement Plan

## Sources Relied On

- `inputs_ai/measurement/engagement_metrics_and_experimentation.md`
- `outputs_ai/wiki/topics/measurement-and-kpis.md`

## Core Metrics

- Reachability rate.
- Response rate.
- Confirmation and rescheduling rate.
- No-show rate where relevant.
- Care gap closure or follow-up completion.
- Human escalation rate.
- Opt-out rate.
- Complaint or trust issue rate.
- Staff burden.
- Safety events or inappropriate automation events.

## AI Workflow Metrics

- Classification accuracy.
- Human override rate.
- Escalation timeliness.
- QA pass rate.
- Unsupported claim or unsafe response count.

## Testing Posture

Start with low-risk pilots, segment results, sample outputs for QA, and review patient trust and accessibility signals before expanding.
