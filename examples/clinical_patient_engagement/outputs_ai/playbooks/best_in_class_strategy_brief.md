## Best Current Direction

The strongest baseline strategy is not a single channel. It is a segmented, consent-aware, closed-loop engagement operating model that uses automation for scale and humans for trust, ambiguity, and exception handling.

## Practical Moves

- Build patient and caregiver context before outreach.
- Use SMS and portal/email for clear low-risk actions.
- Use calls and navigators for repeated non-response, complexity, or trust-sensitive situations.
- Use AI to draft, classify, summarize, route, and monitor.
- Keep vendor claims separate from independent evidence.

## What To Test First

- SMS reminders plus live navigator escalation for high-risk non-response.
- AI reply classification into staff work queues.
- Outreach timeline UI for care coordinators.
