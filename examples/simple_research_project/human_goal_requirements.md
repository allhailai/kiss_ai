# Goal Requirements

## Project Objective

Research a simple local market question and produce a short, practical summary that helps compare options, identify risks, and decide what to investigate next.

## Audience

- A non-technical user testing the `kiss_ai` workflow.
- An AI agent learning the expected project shape.

## In Scope

- Local competitors, customer segments, pricing signals, and useful public sources.
- A small number of source notes in `inputs_human/`.
- Short generated outputs that are easy to review.

## Out of Scope

- Private customer data.
- Paid market databases.
- Legal, financial, or investment advice.
- Large multi-region analysis.

## Decision Criteria

- Which options look promising.
- Which assumptions are weak.
- Which questions need human follow-up.

## Constraints

- Do not invent facts.
- Cite source files for material claims.
- Keep outputs short and plain-language.
- Ask before changing the project scope.
